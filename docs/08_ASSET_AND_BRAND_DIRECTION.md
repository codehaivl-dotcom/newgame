# ART + CONTENT DESIGN — GARDEN PUZZLE CLUB

## Art identity, not four direct copies
Premium calm illustration: ivory backgrounds, botanical green and warm wood; clear high-contrast tiles, gentle shadows, bright but non-neon gem/ball colors. Consistent soft rounded board language across 4 modes. Subtle nature achievement albums: flower postcard, home garden, scenic France/Vietnam botanical culture optional, not an exploitative "grandma" stereotype. All designs original, no copyrighted title/game asset imitation.

## Asset acceptance tests
- At actual 360px phone viewport, target tile distinct with shapes besides colors; no microscopic details that disappear.
- Characters not needed. Don't generate humanoids just to consume existing 3D credits.
- 1 asset / source image unless a tested crop sheet is explicitly approved; clean transparency or neutral background. Use family palette and consistent stroke width.
- No score/levels baked into illustration, no translated text inside pictures; localization lives in UI.
- 4 backgrounds never obscure board. Parallax/camera shake disabled, "low motion" supported.
- Avoid visual clutter, giant HUD and popups. Major gameplay state must remain visible after 1s.
- Archive rejected render and record why, not fake PASS. All generated image paths must be real and verified.
